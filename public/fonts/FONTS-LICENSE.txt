Fonts bundled with Keatland (self-hosted, Latin subset, so the site works offline and sends no requests to third parties):

* Permanent Marker (Font Diner) — brush/marker caps for the rare big moments and headings. Apache License 2.0 (full text: LICENSE-Apache-2.0-PermanentMarker.txt).
* Special Elite (Astigmatic) — typewriter for body text, captions and whispers. Apache License 2.0 (full text: LICENSE-Apache-2.0-SpecialElite.txt).
* Rubik Wet Paint (The Rubik Filtered Project Authors) — the dripping "Keatland" title and painted page signs. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (full text: OFL-RubikWetPaint.txt).

All downloaded from the Google Fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts) and subset with fonttools.
* Rye (Nicole Fally) — the western display face on the Keatland Motors / Freedomland signs. SIL OFL 1.1 (OFL-rye.txt).
* Oswald (Vernon Adams et al.) — condensed sans for parking, facilities and office signage. SIL OFL 1.1 (OFL-oswald.txt).
* VT323 (Peter Hull) — the dot-matrix announcement boards under Yesterkeat's ceiling speakers. SIL OFL 1.1 (OFL-vt323.txt).
* Caveat (Impallari Type) — handwriting on sticky notes, the clipboard prophecy and Dale's shouting. SIL OFL 1.1 (OFL-caveat.txt).
  (These four ship as Latin-subset .ttf.)
